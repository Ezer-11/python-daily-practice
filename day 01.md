Day 1 — Basic Logic & Number Manipulation
Q1. Largest of Three Numbers
- Take three integers as input.
- Find and print the largest number.
- Do not use max().
  
Q2. Positive, Negative and Zero Counter
- Take multiple integers as input.
- Count how many are positive, negative and zero.
- Print all three counts.
  
Q3. Reverse a Number
- Take an integer as input.
- Reverse its digits mathematically.
- Do not convert the number into a string.
  
Q4. Second-Largest Distinct Element
- Take a list of integers.
- Find the second-largest distinct element.
- Do not use sort() or sorted().
- Handle duplicates and cases where no second-largest element exists.
  
Q5. Perfect Number
- Take a positive integer.
- Check whether it is a perfect number.
- A perfect number equals the sum of its positive divisors excluding itself.
- Example: 6 = 1 + 2 + 3.
