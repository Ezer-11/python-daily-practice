
DAY 04 — PYTHON LOGIC PRACTICE (loops)

Q1. FACTORIAL OF A NUMBER

Take a non-negative integer n and calculate its factorial using a loop.

Factorial means multiplying all positive integers from 1 to n.
By definition, 0! = 1.

Input:
5

Output:
120

Q2. MULTIPLICATION TABLE
Difficulty: Beginner

Take an integer and print its multiplication table from 1 to 10.

Input:
7

Output:
7 x 1 = 7
7 x 2 = 14
7 x 3 = 21
...
7 x 10 = 70

Q3. ROTATE A LIST TO THE RIGHT BY ONE POSITION

Take a list of integers and move its last element to the first position.
Every other element should shift one position to the right.

Input:
5
10 20 30 40 50

Output:
50 10 20 30 40


Q4. FIND THE FREQUENCY OF EVERY ELEMENT

Take a list of integers and count how many times each distinct number occurs.

Input:
8
2 3 2 5 3 2 5 7

Output:
2 occurs 3 times
3 occurs 2 times
5 occurs 2 times
7 occurs 1 time

Requirement:
Use a Python dictionary.
Do not use count().


Q5. FIND ALL PRIME NUMBERS IN A RANGE

Take two integers L and R.
Print all prime numbers between them, including the boundaries if they are prime.

A prime number is greater than 1 and has exactly two positive divisors:
1 and itself.

Input:
10 30

Output:
11 13 17 19 23 29
